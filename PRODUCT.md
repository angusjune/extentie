# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chrome users who manage enough extensions or apps to benefit from searching, grouping, and quickly enabling or disabling them from the browser toolbar.

## Product Purpose

Extentie makes routine extension management fast and unobtrusive in a compact popup, while providing a larger setup surface for preferences and custom groups.

## Brand Personality

Simple, elegant, and calm. The interface should feel native to Chrome and stay out of the user's way.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense administration panels, decorative controls, unfamiliar interaction patterns, and visual treatments that compete with extension names or primary actions.

## Design Principles

- Keep frequent actions close to the content they affect.
- Prefer familiar Chrome and Material interaction patterns.
- Preserve useful information density without making the popup feel crowded.
- Make state changes immediate, predictable, and persistent.
- Keep the experience coherent across light mode, dark mode, and supported locales.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus behavior, accessible names for icon-only controls, and sufficient contrast in both color schemes. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
